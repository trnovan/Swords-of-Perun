ABOUT

SWORDS OF PERUN is an original retro first-person action game set in a dark Slavic pagan world. The old shrines burn, the dead walk out of the marshes, and something ancient stirs beneath the temple of the Old Gods.

You are a sworn warrior of the thunder god. Your sword is etched with runes older than the villages you defend. Cut your way through the burning village, the forest of the Leshy and the underground temple — and face the Ancient Idol Guardian.

All art, sound and code in this game are procedurally generated and original.
